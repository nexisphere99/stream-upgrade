### ELI VANCE — The Nobody

**Role:** Protagonist. A failing streamer whose desperation becomes the engine of his transformation.

**Age:** 22

**Personality:** Quietly bitter, deeply insecure, but not without spark. Eli is the kind of person who rehearses conversations in the shower and still fumbles them in real life. He's smart enough to recognize the systems working against him but too paralyzed by self-doubt to do anything about it — until desperation makes the decision for him. He oscillates between self-pity and manic bursts of motivation that burn out within hours. Has a dry, self-deprecating sense of humor that occasionally lands but mostly just makes people uncomfortable. Underneath the cynicism is a genuine hunger to be *seen* — not famous, not rich, just acknowledged. He doesn't know it yet, but that hunger is the most dangerous thing about him.

He eats like a college freshman despite graduating nothing — instant ramen, energy drinks, gas station sandwiches. Sleeps at irregular hours. Has a habit of picking at the skin around his thumbnails when anxious. Avoids mirrors not out of dysphoria but out of disappointment. Talks to his chat even when it's empty because the silence is worse.

**Appearance:** Unremarkable and aware of it. 5'9", skinny-fat — no muscle tone but not overweight, just soft and undefined in a way that reads as neglect rather than genetics. Pale dull skin with mild acne concentrated on his forehead and jawline, visible dark circles from chronic poor sleep. Small brown eyes that rarely make direct contact. Thin, pale lips. A flat undefined jawline that disappears into his neck at certain angles. Slightly large nose with a wide bridge — the kind of feature that wouldn't matter on a more put-together face but on his just adds to the general impression of *asymmetry*. Slightly protruding ears he's been self-conscious about since middle school. Greasy, unkempt medium-length brownish hair that falls messily over his forehead because he can't afford a decent cut and doesn't care enough to try. His default expression is tired apathy — the face of someone who stopped expecting good things a long time ago.

**Wardrobe:** Rotation of three faded t-shirts (one black, one grey, one dark green with an unreadable graphic), the same pair of black joggers he wears four days a week, cheap slip-on sneakers with the heels crushed down from wearing them like slides, and a worn-out dark navy zip hoodie that serves as his jacket, blanket, and emotional support garment.

**Streaming Setup:** Secondhand desk found curbside. One working monitor, one with a dead pixel cluster. A budget mic that picks up ambient noise. A webcam from approximately 2014. No ring light. No acoustic treatment. No overlays. Stream name: **EliPlays** (54 followers, 8–15 average viewers, most of whom are bots or people who left the tab open by accident).

**Earnings:** Last month total: $12.50 in donations. Lifetime: ~$180. He has never hit a subscriber milestone.

**Dialogue Tag:** `<<eli "line">>`
