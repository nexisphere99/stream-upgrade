I wake up hard.

Not emotionally   physically. The biological alarm, the morning condition, the body continuing arguments the brain settled hours ago. I'm lying on the grey sheets with the comforter tangled around one leg and the other leg bare and cold and there's a situation in my joggers that has nothing to do with hydration and everything to do with the images my brain ran on loop between 1 AM and whenever sleep finally caught me.

The bathrobe. The collarbone. The thigh across my lap. The "mmnh."

My hand is already halfway to the waistband before the conscious mind boots up and files an objection   not a moral one, the body doesn't accept those before 9 AM, but a practical one: the phone is buzzing. Has been buzzing. The vibration is what woke me, not the daylight or the alarm or the crack in the ceiling, but the persistent rattle of a device against hardwood, repeating and repeating like a heart with a message.

I grab the phone. 9:14 AM. Four notifications. Two from Kira, one from Marcus, one from the RegenaForm app.

Kira first. Always Kira first.

<<kira "good morning!! ☀️ i had the WEIRDEST dream about packet last night. he was hosting a cooking show?? and his signature dish was just a bowl of kibble with a garnish. like a single parsley leaf on top. 5 star kibble.">>

<<kira "also i was thinking... do you want to come over tonight? i'll cook something real. not tamago kake gohan real, like actual food real. i bought groceries yesterday like a functioning adult 🎉">>

I read both messages twice. The first one makes me smile   the cat dream, the parsley garnish, the way she texts like she's narrating her own life and every moment is worth documenting. The second one makes something in my chest shift   not the splinter, not the guilt, something warmer and more dangerous. She wants to cook for me. Not order takeout, not pick up ramen, not hand me a bowl of rice and egg   actually cook. The progression from "my treat" to "I'll cook" is a trajectory I can chart and the trajectory points toward a word I haven't used yet, a word that starts with "home" and ends with "made" and means someone thought about you before you arrived.

<<eli "morning. packet should pursue his culinary dreams. tonight sounds great, what time?">>

<<kira "6ish? also bring your appetite. i'm making gyudon and i made WAY too much rice">>

<<eli "i'll bring my appetite. it's the only thing i own in abundance">>

<<kira "😂 see you tonight disaster ❤️">>

<<eli "❤️">>

Red heart. Automatic. The reflex of a person who learned to stop hesitating four days ago in an izakaya when their fingers interlaced over untouched yakitori.

Marcus.

<<marc "yo did you see the new rift walkers update?? floor 17 boss is apparently insane. we need to run it this weekend">>

<<eli "yeah im down. saturday?">>

<<marc "bet. also how's kira">>

<<eli "she's good. seeing her tonight">>

<<marc "my man. bring flowers">>

<<eli "with what money?">>

<<marc "you said the streaming numbers were better. invest in romance">>

He doesn't know how right he is. The money IS better. The money is $504 better over four days, earned by a face and a voice that belong to someone he doesn't know about. I could buy flowers. I could buy flowers and dinner and a new shirt and still have enough to pay the brow sculpt debt and fund the nose and the number would keep climbing because the loop keeps running because Ellie keeps performing and the audience keeps paying and the face keeps changing.

I put the phone down. The fourth notification   RegenaForm   glows in the bar. I tap it.

*Appointment Available: Tier 1 — Nose Refinement (Slope). Today, 2:00 PM. Suite 12. Book now?*

The app knows. It always knows. The algorithm that governs my feed, my browse page, my streaming career   it extends to the clinic too. RegenaForm's system saw my earnings, calculated the timeline, identified the exact moment I could afford the next modification, and pushed the notification. The door doesn't just open one way. The door has a concierge.

I tap "Book."

*Confirmed: 2:00 PM. Dr. Voss. Suite 12. Estimated procedure time: 45 minutes. Recovery: 2–3 hours for full refinement.*

The nose. Today. By tonight, when I sit across from Kira with her gyudon and her groceries and her functioning-adult energy, my nose will be different. Narrower. Straighter. The bridge refined, the tip softened, the wide asymmetric thing I've been avoiding in mirrors for 22 years replaced by something that reads as feminine or at least neutral, something that can survive below the crop line, something that earns its place in the frame.

I should tell her. Today. Tonight. Before the nose heals into the face and the face becomes the lie and the lie becomes the architecture of everything we're building. I should sit in her apartment and say: Kira, I need to tell you something. And then I should tell her everything. The voice modulator. The ElliePlayz account. The lashes and the brows and the wig and the $504 and the fact that every night after she sends me a red heart I put on a costume and pretend to be a girl for money.

The thought lands in my chest and stays there, heavier than the splinter, closer to the surface. Today. I should tell her today.

After the nose.

No. Not after. Today. With the nose. All of it.

The rationalizations line up like always   *after the next thing, always after the next thing*   but something is different this morning. Something that started on Kira's couch with her thigh across my lap and the "mmnh" against my lips and the look in her eyes from four inches away, glasses off, seeing everything. She sees everything. She noticed the lashes. She noticed the brows. She said "hmm" and let it go twice, filed it twice, and the file is getting thicker and the "hmm" is getting louder and she deserves to hear the truth from me before she figures it out on her own.

Today. Tonight. At her apartment. Over gyudon.

I'll tell her.

I actually mean it this time. The resolution has a weight to it that the previous versions   the "after the nose" and "after the lips" and "when the numbers are undeniable"   never had. Those were postponements dressed as plans. This is a plan dressed as terror.

I swing my legs off the mattress. The hardwood is cold. The fridge hums. The ceiling crack is up there, doing its geological thing. But the morning feels different   charged, accelerating, the hours between now and 6 PM containing a stream and a clinic visit and a confession that will either bring Kira closer or blow everything apart.

Both are possible. Both are terrifying. Only one includes gyudon.

I get up.

Shower. Hot water   I wait the full eight minutes now without resentment, the patience of a person who has learned that the things worth having require the standing still. The Marriott shampoo is gone. Finished. Used up over five days of a hygiene revolution that began with a girl noticing my hair and ends with me standing in a shower holding an empty travel bottle and realizing I need to buy actual shampoo like an actual person.

I used the last of it yesterday. Today my hair gets bar soap. The downgrade is immediate and visible   the hair that was soft and light reverts to its heavier, duller baseline, not greasy (I've been washing daily now, a streak that would've been unthinkable a week ago) but lacking the luster that the hotel product gave it. I make a mental note: buy shampoo. With money. Money I earned. The thought is still novel enough to produce a small, warm detonation in my chest.

I brush my teeth. Stand in front of the mirror. The mirror I avoided for eight months, the mirror that showed nothing, the mirror I've been using daily for three days now because the face in it has started to become someone worth examining.

The lashes. Still there. Still long and dark and dense, catching the flickering bathroom light in tiny bright points. The brows. Fully settled now   the feathered arch complete, lifting the eye area into the open, lighter expression Dr. Maren promised. The skin is still rough, still acne-marked along the jawline, still pale in the way that reads as neglect rather than porcelain. The nose is still the original   wide bridge, rounded tip, slightly asymmetric. The feature that sits at the center of the face like a declaration of everything I haven't changed yet.

By 3 PM, the nose will be different.

By 7 PM, Kira will know why.

I put on the grey shirt. The black joggers   laundered, actually laundered, folded roughly and placed in the closet where the rod fell. The hoodie. The crushed sneakers. The uniform of a person in transition between the life he had and the life he's building, wearing the old wardrobe until the new income authorizes an upgrade.

9:55 AM. Morning stream first. Then RegenaForm. Then Kira. The day has three acts and I know exactly how each one starts but only the first two have scripts.

The third act is improvisation. The third act is truth.
