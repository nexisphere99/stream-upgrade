# Stream Upgrade: Ellie.exe — Interactive Visual Novel
## Game Design Document — Part 1: Core Systems & Act 1

---

## 1. GAME OVERVIEW

**Genre:** Interactive Visual Novel / Life Simulation / Erotic Transformation
**Perspective:** Second person ("You open the stream dashboard...")
**Core Loop:** Stream → Earn → Transform → Unlock new content/interactions → Stream again
**Tone:** Starts comedic/anxious, escalates into erotic, psychological, and dark territory

---

## 2. CORE SYSTEMS

### 2.1 Currency & Economy

| Currency | Source | Use |
|----------|--------|-----|
| **Dollars ($)** | Donations, subscriptions, tips during live streams | RegenaForm procedures, equipment, cosmetics |
| **Clout** | Viewer milestones, viral moments, fan satisfaction | Unlocks new stream types, fan interaction tiers |
| **Desire Index (DI)** | How attracted fans are; rises with transformations + interactions | Unlocks explicit content requests, physical meetups |

**Earning rates scale with:**
- Current appearance (more feminine = more donations)
- Stream type (gaming < reaction < Just Chatting < Lingerie < Explicit Private)
- Fan engagement choices (flirty chat, fulfilling requests, teasing)
- Active transformation tier (each new mod multiplies base income)

### 2.2 Transformation Tiers

Each tier is a GATE. You cannot skip tiers. Each tier has multiple OPTIONS within it (size, shape, color, style). Costs escalate.

| Tier | Category | Unlocks | Base Cost ($) |
|------|----------|---------|----------------|
| T0 | **Baseline Eli** | Basic gaming streams | 0 |
| T1 | **Facial Feminization** | Softened jaw, cheekbones, lips, eyes, skin | 500–2,000 |
| T2 | **Voice Modulation** | Feminine vocal range, breathy options | 1,500 |
| T3 | **Hair Redesign** | Length, color, texture, style | 800–1,500 |
| T4 | **Body Contour: Waist** | Narrowed waist, hip suggestion | 3,000–5,000 |
| T5 | **Breasts** | Multiple sizes: A-cup through G-cup | 5,000–15,000 |
| T6 | **Buttocks & Hips** | Shape, size, firmness options | 5,000–12,000 |
| T7 | **Full Lower Feminization** | Female reproductive system | 20,000–40,000 |
| T8 | **Neural/Hormonal Integration** | Full sensory mapping, hormonal cycle | 50,000+ |

**Within each tier, sub-options:**

**T1 — Facial Feminization (mix and match):**
- Jawline: Soft, Angular-Feminine, Heart-Shaped
- Lips: Natural-Full, Plush, Bee-Stung
- Eyes: Wider-Set, Cat-Eye, Doe-Eye

**T5 — Breasts:**
- Size: A (subtle), B (modest), C (noticeable), D (attention-grabbing), DD/E (dominant), F+ (extreme)
- Shape: Teardrop, Round, Perky, Natural-Hang

**T6 — Buttocks & Hips:**

- Shape: Round, Heart, Shelf, Bubble

**T7 — Full Lower Feminization:**

- Full functional (internal + external, sensation-complete)
- "Dual configuration" — retain original + add new (costly, risky, unique branch)

### 2.3 Appearance Score

A composite rating (0–100) that determines what content/interactions are available:

| Score Range | Label | What's Unlocked |
|-------------|-------|-----------------|
| 0–15 | Eli (Male Default) | Basic gaming streams |
| 16–30 | Androgynous | "Femboy" niche audience, mild curiosity |
| 31–50 | Passing (Face) | Dating app interactions, kiss-for-cash, facecam streams |
| 51–65 | Convincingly Female (Upper Body) | Cleavage requests, private cam shows, physical groping encounters |
| 66–80 | Fully Feminine (Torso) | Topless private streams, physical breast play, blowjob encounters |

| 91–100 | Indistinguishable / "Perfect Ellie" | Full vaginal sex, complete girlfriend experience, all content unlocked |

Each transformation tier adds to this score. Player choices about SIZE and STYLE within each tier also affect the score and shift fan demographics.

### 2.4 Fan Types

| Fan Type | Attracted By | Requests | Payment Style |
|----------|-------------|----------|---------------|
| **Curious Lurkers** | Early changes, androgyny | "Show your face," basic compliments | Small tips |
| **Simps** | Beauty, perceived vulnerability | Dates, personal attention | Steady subscriptions |
| **Whales** | Exclusivity, explicit access | Private shows, meetups, sex | Massive one-time drops |
| **Fetishists** | Transformation itself | Progress pics, "before/after," specific mods | Niche but loyal |
| **Stalkers** | Obsession, parasocial delusion | Increasingly invasive demands | Unpredictable, sometimes dangerous |
| **Romantics** | Emotional connection | Real dates, genuine relationship | Moderate, consistent |

Fan satisfaction drives Clout and Desire Index. Ignoring dominant fan types causes subscriber drops.

## 4. ACT 1 — "THE EXPERIMENT" (Detailed Branch Map)

### 4.1 Opening: Chapter 0 — "Dead Channel"

**Scene:** Eli's cramped apartment. Dual monitors. 12 viewers on his stream. He's playing a competitive shooter badly. Chat is dead except for one bot and a guy named `xX_DarkSlayer_Xx` saying "ur trash lol."

**Choice 1: How does Eli react to the dead chat?**
- **[A] Rage quit and rant** → +Frustration stat, leads to angry internet browsing, discovers RegenaForm through a rage-click rabbit hole
- **[B] Quietly end stream, stare at ceiling** → +Desperation stat, discovers RegenaForm through a "how to get more viewers" forum post at 3 AM
- **[C] Try harder, put on a show** → +Determination stat, notices a female streamer with 10k viewers doing the same game; discovers RegenaForm through her chat mentioning "she wasn't always this hot"

All three paths converge at: **Eli finds the RegenaForm website.**

### 4.2 Chapter 1 — "The Consultation"

**Scene:** RegenaForm's sleek, unsettling clinic. Dr. Maren — calm, beautiful, ambiguously gendered — explains the tech. DNA-based bioengineering. No scars. Full sensory integration. "We don't change you. We unlock you."

**Choice 2: Eli's initial attitude at the clinic:**
- **[A] Skeptical but desperate** → "Just enough to look better on camera. Nothing weird."
- **[B] Excited, already fantasizing** → "What's the full menu? How far can this go?"
- **[C] Nervous, almost backs out** → Dr. Maren offers a free "sample" — a subtle skin smoothing — to hook him

This choice sets Eli's **Internal Resistance Meter** (high/medium/low), which affects how quickly he psychologically adapts to each transformation.

### 4.3 Chapter 2 — "New Face"

**Tier 1 Transformation: Facial Feminization**

Player selects facial modifications from the sub-options menu (jawline, lips, eyes, skin, nose). Each combination produces a different composite appearance and costs differently.

**Scene:** Eli wakes up post-procedure. Looks in the mirror. The face staring back is... him? Her? Something magnetic.

**First Stream After T1:**

Viewer count: 12 → 85 (immediate spike). Chat starts buzzing:
- "wait is this a new streamer?"
- "you look different, eli... good different"
- "are you wearing makeup??"
- "cute af ngl"

**Choice 3: How does Eli address the chat's reaction?**
- **[A] Deflect — "Just got a skincare routine lol"** → Chat buys it. Slow build. Conservative path.
- **[B] Lean in — "You like? Maybe I'll keep upgrading ;)"** → Chat explodes. Fast fan growth. Flirty path.
- **[C] Stay silent about it, just play the game** → Mysterious aura. Chat starts theorizing. Enigma path.

**Post-Stream Scene:** Eli checks the numbers. More donations in one stream than the last three months combined. His phone buzzes — RegenaForm's app shows a message: "Ready for the next step? Voice modulation consultation available. -Dr. Maren"

### 4.4 Chapter 3 — "Her Voice"

**Tier 2 Transformation: Voice Modulation**

Player selects voice type:
- **Sweet/Breathy** — Classic "e-girl" voice. Maximum simp appeal.
- **Husky/Low-Feminine** — Sultry, mature. Attracts older, wealthier fans.
- **Bright/Energetic** — Bubbly streamer voice. Broad appeal, younger demographic.

**Scene:** The procedure is quick. Eli speaks for the first time afterward. The sound that comes out isn't his. It's hers. He says his own name and it sounds like someone else entirely.

**Pivotal psychological moment — Choice 4: Eli's reaction to hearing the new voice:**
- **[A] Aroused** → First hint of autogynephilic attraction. Eli whispers into the mic at home, listens back, feels a heat he can't explain. (+Desire Index, +Internal Acceptance)
- **[B] Panicked** → "That's not me. That's NOT me." Calls Dr. Maren asking if it's reversible. She says yes but recommends "living with it for a week." He agrees reluctantly. (+Anxiety, +Resistance)
- **[C] Pragmatic** → "Okay. This is a business decision. Let's see what it does to the numbers." Cold calculation. (+Ambition, −Emotional Vulnerability)

**Second Major Stream (Post-Voice):**

Viewer count: 85 → 340.

Chat goes wild:
- "WAIT. YOUR VOICE. WHAT."
- "queen??? is that you???"
- "I literally just subbed"
- "say my name please 🥺"

**Fan Interaction Event 1: "Say My Name"**
A fan donates $50 and requests Eli say their name in the new voice.

**Choice 5:**
- **[A] Do it playfully** — "Aww, [name], thanks for the donation~" (breathy, cute) → +Clout, +Simp fanbase, opens "Voice Request" income stream
- **[B] Do it but feel weird about it** — Eli does it, stream loves it, but afterward stares at the VOD replay feeling something between pride and disgust → +Internal Conflict subplot
- **[C] Refuse** — "Haha no that's weird" → Chat respects boundary but donations slow. Some unsub. (-$ income, +Self-Respect stat)

### 4.5 Chapter 4 — "The First Request"

**Scene:** Between streams. Eli checks DMs. Among the usual noise, a message from `MidnightWhale99`:

> "Hey Ellie (can I call you that?). You're incredible. I've been watching since the beginning. I'd pay serious money to see more of you. Not gaming. Just... you. Your face. Up close. Maybe a selfie? I'll send $200."

**Choice 6: The First Explicit-Adjacent Request**
- **[A] Send a selfie (face only, cute angle)** → $200 earned. Opens "Private Content" income branch. MidnightWhale becomes a recurring character. +Clout, +Desire Index
- **[B] Send a selfie but feel conflicted** → Same income, but triggers an internal monologue: "Am I selling myself? Or am I selling a character?" → +Story Depth, unlocks "Identity Crisis" subplot early
- **[C] Decline politely** → "Thanks but I don't do that stuff haha" → MidnightWhale respects it... for now. Resurfaces later with bigger offers.
- **[D] Decline harshly** → "Creep." → MidnightWhale becomes antagonistic. Seeds for Stalker subplot.

### 4.6 Act 1 Branching Summary

By the end of Act 1, the player has established:

1. **Eli's psychological profile** (Resistant / Accepting / Calculated)
2. **Streamer persona** (Conservative / Flirty / Mysterious)
3. **Income trajectory** (Slow-Steady / Boom / Volatile)
4. **Fan relationship dynamics** (Boundaries set vs. doors opened)
5. **First fan interaction precedent** (Where the line is drawn — or isn't)

**Act 1 ends with the CATALYST moment:**

Eli's viewer count has stabilized around 300–500. Income is decent but not life-changing. Then, during a stream, the highest donation Eli has ever received drops:

> **$1,000 from BreastManBillionaire:** "Love the face. Love the voice. But chat wants to know... when are we getting cleavage? 😏👀"

Chat erupts. "CLEAVAGE ARC CLEAVAGE ARC." A poll goes up in chat — 89% vote yes.

Eli stares at the camera. The stream is watching. RegenaForm's app pings silently on his phone.
