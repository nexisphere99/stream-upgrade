## HOW THIS DOCUMENT WORKS

Every transformation tier is fully detailed with:
- All selectable sub-options with cost, appearance score, and gameplay effects
- Combo bonuses (certain combinations across tiers stack for bonus effects)
- Recovery time (downtime between procedure and next stream)
- **IMAGE PROMPTS** for character sheet generation (new in this version)

**Image Prompt System:**
- Each tier is reordered from **least feminine to most feminine** for incremental progression
- Sub-options within each feature are also ordered least → most dramatic
- Prompts build **cumulatively** — each step stacks on top of the previous
- **Three views** for body tiers: Front, Back, Side (all nude, neck down, no face, single figure)
- **Face-only** for T1 (close-up, head and neck only)
- **Crotch view** for T7 (detail framing)
- **Cross-section diagrams** for T7 internal anatomy
- Non-visual features (sensitivity, scent, etc.) are noted as **[Text Descriptor]**

**Prompt Conventions:**
- All prompts end with: `One person only. Visual novel style, semi-realistic anime, soft cel shading, flat lighting.`
- Front view: `Front-facing [...] against a plain solid black background [...] neutral standing pose, arms at sides, neck to toe, head cropped.`
- Back view: `Rear-facing [...] against a plain solid black background [...] neutral standing pose, arms at sides, neck to toe, head cropped.`
- Side view: `Direct side profile [...] against a plain solid black background [...] facing directly left, perfectly flat side-on view with no rotation or angle, neck to toe, head cropped.`

Options within a tier are MIX AND MATCH unless stated otherwise. Some options within a tier are mutually exclusive (marked with ⚔️). Some have prerequisites (marked with 🔗).
