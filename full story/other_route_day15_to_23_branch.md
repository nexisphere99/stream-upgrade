### The Void
The central feature of the non-Marcus Other Route is the void — the space where a partner would be. In the Kira Route, Kira fills it with warmth and the compass question. In the Marcus Route, Marcus fills it with presence and dumplings. In this route, the void is the dominant geography. The modifications happen alone. The recovery happens alone. The mirror is the only witness.

The three branches offer three answers to the void:
- **Sophie** fills it with vision — she sees the construction and finds it beautiful
- **Jason** fills it with narrative — he sees the story and wants to tell it
- **Solo** leaves it empty — and the emptiness becomes its own kind of story, the darkest one

### Why Day 23?
The branch happens here because Day 23 is the moment the body transformation begins to require a witness. The face could be done alone — a face is a thing you carry into the world and the world reacts to it passively. But the body is different. The body is intimate. The body is the territory of partners, of people who touch you, of hands that map the new geography. Starting the body transformation without a witness is sustainable for a few modifications (throat, hands). But the waist, the breasts, the hips — these are changes that exist in the space between the self and another person. They demand to be seen. They demand a response.

The three paths are three responses to the demand:
- Sophie responds with an image (the photograph)
- Jason responds with words (the narrative)
- Solo responds with silence (the void)

The face was built for the algorithm. The body needs to be built for a person. Day 23 is the day Eli encounters the three possible persons — or the absence of them — and the encounter determines everything that follows.
