## DAY 4 — "The Machine"

### Morning — Engineering Ellie
Wakes at 8:30 AM without an alarm. Studies female streamer engagement techniques on YouTube for 90 minutes — a deliberate, methodical approach:

1. **Eye contact**: Look at the webcam lens, not the chat window. Creates intimacy.
2. **The reaction gasp**: Wide eyes, hand to chest, slightly exaggerated genuine surprise. Lashes amplify everything.
3. **The lean-in**: Sit forward when reading donations. Creates closeness.
4. **The hair touch**: Tuck strands behind ear, sweep over shoulder. Fills dead time with visual content.
5. **Eye acting**: Slow blink for emphasis. Looking up-and-right for "cute thinking face." Wide eyes for excitement.
6. **Vocal performance**: Rising intonation, strategic giggling, the "cute sneeze," "oh!" gasps layered on top of VoxMorph.
7. **Parasocial calibration**: Close enough to feel personal, distant enough to feel aspirational.

"I am engineering a person. Not just a face, not just a voice, but a complete performed identity."
