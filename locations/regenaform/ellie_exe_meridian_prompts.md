# THE MERIDIAN — RegenaForm Transformation Gallery
## Image Generation Prompts

**Purpose:** The Meridian is RegenaForm's immersive showroom — the place where clients browse the full catalog of available transformations across all tiers. Part museum, part configurator, part seduction. It's where desire meets possibility, where clients see what their body could become, and where the escalation from "just eyelashes" to "full reconstruction" begins in earnest. Named for the meridian line — the dividing line between one state and another, the boundary between before and after.

The overall impression is a cathedral of transformation — reverential, overwhelming, designed to make the visitor feel small in the presence of possibility and hungry for the version of themselves they see reflected in the dark glass walls.
