RegenaForm appeared two years ago, quietly, the way all dangerous things appear — a website, a waiting list, a whisper network among streamers and influencers and the body-conscious edges of the internet. No Super Bowl ad. No billboard campaign. Just a chrome double-helix logo and a six-word tagline that embedded itself in the collective consciousness like a splinter:

*We don't change you. We unlock you.*

The technology behind RegenaForm is proprietary, patented, and classified at a level that makes pharmaceutical companies look transparent. What's public is this: they use the client's own DNA as a template. Every modification — every reshaped bone, every restructured tissue, every new configuration of flesh and nerve — is grown from the client's genetic material, activated by a bioactive compound that switches on dormant developmental pathways in existing cells. The body doesn't reject the changes because the changes *are* the body. There's nothing foreign. Nothing synthetic. Nothing that a blood test or a biopsy would flag as artificial.

The results are immediate. The recovery is hours, not weeks. There are no scars. No suture lines. No telltale marks of any kind — not a stitch, not a seam, not a single visible trace that anything was done at all. A jawline can be reshaped in forty minutes. Cheekbones restructured over a lunch break. Skin cleared, smoothed, re-toned to any shade on the human spectrum in the time it takes to watch a movie. Eyelashes grow from the same follicles they always lived in — just longer, darker, denser, as if the body finally remembered what it was supposed to look like all along.

And that's just the face.

RegenaForm's catalog extends from the cosmetic to the radical, with nothing off the table and no procedure considered too extreme. The technology is omnidirectional — it doesn't favor one configuration over another. It builds. It removes. It reshapes. It reverses. Any body, in any state, to any destination. The menu reads like a choose-your-own-adventure for human flesh.

Voices can be modulated organically — not through software but through actual restructuring of the vocal cords, the larynx, the resonance chambers of the throat and sinuses. A baritone can become a soprano. A soprano can become a baritone. The resulting voice is indistinguishable from one that was born, not built, because the tissue is the client's own, just reconfigured. Body contour — shoulders narrowed or broadened, waists cinched or thickened, hips widened or tapered, ribcages compressed or expanded, fat redistributed along any line the client desires — achieved through skeletal and soft tissue modification that leaves the skin unblemished, the body unmarked, as though the bones had always been this shape.

Breasts can be grown. Not implanted — *grown*. From the client's own mammary tissue, activated and expanded, producing breasts in any size from barely-there to physics-defying. Any shape: teardrop, round, perky, natural. Any sensitivity level, from standard to hyper-responsive, the nerve density calibrated like a dial. Fully functional. Full sensation. Indistinguishable from natal development by any examination — mammogram, biopsy, ultrasound — because they *are* natal development, triggered decades after the original window closed.

And breasts can be removed. Completely. The same technology that activates growth can reverse it — the tissue reabsorbs into the body, the mammary cells deactivate, the skin tightens and smooths until the chest is flat, contoured, masculine or androgynous or whatever the client specifies. No mastectomy scars. No asymmetry. No trace. A person could grow D-cups on a Monday and have a flat chest by Wednesday and no medical scan on Thursday would find evidence of either state. The body simply reorganizes itself around the new instruction, as indifferent to the direction of change as water is to the shape of its container.

Buttocks reshaped — grown fuller or reduced to nothing. Hips flared wide or narrowed to a masculine frame. Waists cinched to proportions that corsets couldn't achieve or thickened to a straight, angular torso. Every modification seamless, scarless, grown from within and removable from within. The body rebuilds itself around the new blueprint, and the new blueprint leaves no evidence of the old one. Nor does the reversal leave evidence of the blueprint before that.

At the far end of the catalog, behind a consultation wall and a financial threshold that filters out everyone except the desperate and the committed: full genital construction. In any direction.

A vulva built from the client's own cells — labia, clitoris, vaginal canal — self-lubricating, fully sensate, connected to a nervous system that was rewired to receive its signals as pleasure, as sensation, as the authentic experience of a body that matches its configuration. A uterus. Ovaries. Fallopian tubes. The complete architecture of a female reproductive system, grown inside a body that was born male, operating on hormones that the body now produces naturally because RegenaForm didn't just add parts — it rewrote the endocrine system from the ground up. Full fertility optional. Menstrual cycling optional. Every subsystem independently configurable, like options on a car.

And in the other direction: a penis and testicles grown from a client's own tissue, on a body that was born without them. Full size configurable — length, girth, proportion. Fully erectile, fully sensate, the nerve mapping calibrated to produce authentic male sexual response from arousal through climax. Testicles that produce testosterone naturally, that manufacture viable sperm if the client desires, that hang in a scrotum grown from labial tissue repurposed so seamlessly that a urologist examining it would find nothing to flag. The prostate, the vas deferens, the complete plumbing of a male reproductive system, assembled inside a body that was born female, indistinguishable from natal anatomy by any test, any scan, any lover's touch.

Both directions are fully reversible. A client could transition from male to female, live in that body for years, and transition back — or to something else entirely. Dual configurations are available: both sets of genitalia, simultaneously, fully functional, the body's endocrine system balanced by RegenaForm's proprietary hormonal calibration to support both without conflict. Switchable configurations exist at the experimental tier — anatomy that shifts between states on command, one set active while the other lies dormant in compressed biological storage, toggled by a neural signal the client learns to control like flexing a muscle.

The technology doesn't care about direction. It doesn't care about origin or destination. It sees the body as raw material — infinitely plastic, infinitely configurable — and the client's desire as the only blueprint that matters. Grow it, shrink it, reshape it, reverse it, start over, try something new. The flesh is clay. RegenaForm provides the kiln.

No surgeon's hands. No operating theatre. No months of recovery, no dilations, no complications. Just a reclining chair, a cool gel, a warmth that spreads, and a body that wakes up different. Complete. Unmarked. As though it had always been this way. And if the client changes their mind — another chair, another gel, another warmth, and the body wakes up different again. And again. And again. The only limit is the balance in the account.

The technology is miraculous. The ethics are a void. RegenaForm doesn't ask why. They don't require therapy letters or diagnostic criteria or the careful gatekeeping that traditional medicine employs. They offer a menu, a price list, and a smile. The client chooses. The client pays. The body changes. What happens to the person inside it — the identity, the psychology, the sense of self that was built on the assumption that the body would stay the same — is not RegenaForm's department.

Their clinics have spread through every major city, quiet storefronts in unremarkable buildings, identified only by the chrome helix on frosted glass. The waiting lists are long. The prices are high but not prohibitive — calibrated precisely to be *just* affordable for a person who's desperate enough, *just* within reach of a person who can see the math working out if they earn enough, stream enough, transform enough, earn more, transform more.

The loop is the product. The escalation is the business model. The first procedure is free.
