Stream Upgrade - ellie.exe
Stream Upgrade
ellie.exe
"A game about desperation, transformation, and the price of being seen."

You are Eli - 22 years old, broke, invisible. You stream to eight viewers on a good night from a mattress on the floor of a 280-square-foot studio. When a mysterious clinic called RegenaForm offers to change everything about you - face, voice, body - for a price paid in stream revenue, the choice feels obvious. Until it isn't.

18+ Adult Gender Transformation Choice-Driven SugarCube / Twine Streaming Culture

The crack in the ceiling looks like a river delta. Eli has been staring at it long enough to name the tributaries - the main fissure runs diagonally from somewhere above the kitchenette to a point directly over the pillow. It's been widening since he moved in eight months ago.

Desk. Two monitors. Refurbished 24-inch main screen. A 19-inch secondary with a cluster of dead pixels in the upper right like a tiny permanent bruise on reality. Budget USB microphone slightly crooked. Webcam from the Obama administration. No ring light. No acoustic panels. No custom emotes. Just the stubborn, stupid refusal to quit that he keeps mistaking for ambition.

Then Kira sends a link. And everything changes.

⚠ This game contains explicit adult content. All characters are 18+.
